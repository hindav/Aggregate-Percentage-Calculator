📊 Aggregate Percentage Calculator

A simple web-based tool to calculate your aggregate percentage from marksheets quickly and accurately. Just enter total marks and obtained marks for subjects or semesters, and the calculator instantly shows your overall percentage.

🚀 Features

✅ Calculate overall percentage easily
✅ Add multiple subjects dynamically
✅ Fast & accurate calculation
✅ Simple and clean UI
✅ Useful for students & academic records

🖥️ How It Works

Enter Total Marks and Obtained Marks.

Add more subjects if needed using Add Subject.

Click Calculate Percentage.

Get your aggregate percentage instantly.
